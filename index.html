About This Map

This map presents the administrative boundaries of Pakistan, showing the country’s provinces, districts, and other administrative areas. These boundaries provide a geographic framework for planning, governance, development activities, and spatial analysis.

The map is intended for visualization and reference purposes and may not represent legally recognized or disputed territorial boundaries.
